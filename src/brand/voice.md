# Brand Guidelines & Voice Guide: VayuDrishti (वायु दृष्टि)

**Working Name:** VayuDrishti (National Air Quality & Monitoring Infrastructure Observatory)  
**Audience:** High court & tribunal judges, municipal commissioners, public-health epidemiologists, civic planners, and citizens.  
**Core Mission:** Provide clear, factual transparency into historical ambient air quality datasets and physical monitoring coverage distributions across India.

---

## 1. Brand Wordmark & Visual Identity

- **Name:** VayuDrishti
- **Tagline:** National Air Quality & Monitoring Infrastructure Observatory
- **Visual Motif:** Atmospheric sensor aperture and concentric radar rings, representing geospatial ground truth and spatial observation.
- **Palette Identity:** Civic Tech Sky Blue (`#0284c7`), Slate Navy (`#0f172a`), with strict decoupling from environmental severity colors.

---

## 2. Voice & Tone Principles

### Principle 1: Plain Language First
- **Do:** Use clear, straightforward English and vernacular terminology that a non-specialist judge or citizen immediately understands.
- **Don't:** Hide behind obscure atmospheric chemistry abbreviations or uncontextualized sensor telemetry jargon without immediate plain-English explanations.

### Principle 2: Honest About Uncertainty (Zero False Reassurance)
- **Do:** Explicitly distinguish between **"clean air"** and **"unmonitored territory"**. If a city has no working sensor within 10 km, explicitly state: *"No monitoring data in this dataset. Closest reporting station in this dataset is 140 km away."*
- **Don't:** Color an unmonitored district green or imply compliance due to absence of data. Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence.

### Principle 3: Factual and Empirical, Never Alarmist
- **Do:** State figures plainly: *"8-hour average PM2.5 is 184 µg/m³, exceeding National Ambient Air Quality Standard (60 µg/m³) by 3.1x."*
- **Don't:** Use sensationalist adjectives like *"apocalyptic"*, *"deadly suffocating smog"*, *"toxic catastrophe"*, or panic-inducing clickbait.

### Principle 4: Disclose Spatial & Temporal Boundaries
- **Do:** Note data period, city-level geocodes, and dataset limits (e.g., *"Data covers 2015-01-01 to 2020-07-01 from CPCB extract; historical dataset, not current live network"*).
- **Don't:** Present localized city-level geocoded points as exact street-level coordinates or claim live sensor streaming.

### Principle 5: Standardized Wording & Prohibited Terms
- **Required Formulation:** Use `"No usable data in this dataset"` instead of `"Silent"` or `"Offline"`.
- **Required Formulation:** Use `"Reporting in dataset"` instead of `"Active"`.
- **Prohibited Words:** Never use `"offline"`, `"broken"`, or `"active"`.
- **Decoupled Semantics:** Never confuse poor air quality (AQI) with poor data completeness.

## 3. Communication Matrix for Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Primary Need | Communication Standard |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Public-Health Officials** | Population exposure estimates, vulnerability hot-spots. | Present population-weighted coverage statistics (e.g. population living within 10 km vs 50 km). |
| **Citizens & Community** | Actionable, accessible health advisories and local truth. | Plain language advisories, clear indicators for stations with no usable data, zero jargon, screen-reader accessible. |
